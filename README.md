# 📊 Sales & Financial Data Analysis – AtliQ Hardware (Excel Project)

This project is part of my data analytics learning journey, where I analyzed **sales and financial performance** of AtliQ Hardware using Microsoft Excel. The analysis covered sales growth, product performance, profitability, and market-level trends — providing actionable business insights through dashboards, pivot tables, and visual storytelling.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The main goal of this project was to:

- Analyze AtliQ Hardware’s sales and financial performance over 2020–2021  
- Identify top-performing customers, countries, and products  
- Evaluate revenue gaps vs targets and profitability by division and market  
- Build Excel-based reports to present key trends and findings visually  

---

## 🔧 Tools Used

- **Microsoft Excel**  
- **Pivot Tables** – For dynamic analysis and summarization  
- **Charts** – Bar, column, line, and pie for data visualization  
- **Conditional Formatting** – Highlighting patterns and variances  
- **Excel Functions** – For data cleaning and metric calculations  
- **Dashboards** – To present interactive and executive-level summaries  

---

## 🛠️ Key Features

- ✅ **Customer Net Sales Performance**: Despite the pandemic, 2021 sales surged, with some customers growing over **300%**  
- ✅ **Market vs Target**: 2021 revenue hit **$598.9M**, but fell short of target by **$54.9M** (**-8.4%**)  
- ✅ **Top 5 Countries (2021)**: Contributed **$367.2M**, forming a major share of total sales  
- ✅ **Top 10 Products**: Grew from **$6.4M to $52.0M** (an **808% increase**) with AQ Smash 2 and AQ Electron 4 leading  
- ✅ **Division-Level Report**:  
  - PC Division: +**413.7%**  
  - P&A Division: +**321.53%**  
  - N&S Division: +**94.7%**  
- ✅ **Top vs Bottom Products (By Quantity)**: AQ Gamers Ms significantly outperformed others at **$51.71M**  
- ✅ **New Products in 2021**: 16 new launches totaled **$176.2M** in revenue  
- ✅ **P&L Analysis by Fiscal Year**: Strong growth in net sales and gross margin, with a slight drop in margin %  
- ✅ **P&L by Fiscal Month**: Quarterly profits steadily increased  
- ✅ **Market-wise P&L**: India led in net sales (**$161.3M**) while countries like France and New Zealand had higher margins (>45%)
